EASA COM­MONS

BINGO HAVEL participated in the 2023 edition of EASA in Sheffield. Together with Phoebe Nickols we developed a workshop that defined skills as a common resource to be shared and celebrated. Looking back on the industrial history of Sheffield we used bricklaying to examine how a decline in skilled trades affects the future of construction in the UK and Europe. Our aim was to construct a beautiful and functional masonry intervention for a local group. 15 students from all over Europe and the world got their hands dirty and provided the local communitygroup Friends of Abbeyfield Park with a Bread and Pizzaoven.
