SAUNA ALPBACH

The small hut forms an additional room of the house. In its expression it draws on the vocabulary already found on site: colours, motifs and forms are taken from the existing buildings. Two steps lead down to the door of the sauna, which is sunk into the topography to reduce its presence in the view from the terrace. A wooden bench offers room for two to three people sitting or one person lying down. A window frames the view of the surrounding mountain landscape.

Alpbach, Austria, 2025
