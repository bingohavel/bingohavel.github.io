ALPBACH

The small hut does not see itself as a standalone object but as one more room of the house. In its expression it draws on the vocabulary already found on site: colours, motifs and forms are taken from the wonderful world of the existing buildings.

Two steps lead down to the door of the sauna, which is sunk into the topography and so tries to withdraw from the view from the terrace.

A wooden bench offers room for two to three people sitting or one person lying down. A window frames the view of the surrounding mountain landscape.

Alpbach, Austria, 2025
