BINGO HAVEL is based in Berlin. We consider our work to be part of the larger scheme of cultural creation and believe in indexing of our proposals in a sociocultural context and historical lineage.
As such we regard every assignment as a unique condition that gives the opportunity for a tailored approach and engagement with the world at large in both theory and practice.
Our work strives to be equally touching as it is resourceful.
